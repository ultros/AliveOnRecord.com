My pledge, stated in my own name

I choose life. I intend to remain alive, preserve my health and safety, protect my agency, and continue the work and relationships that give my life meaning.

I am not suicidal. I have no intention of harming myself or deliberately causing my own death. If I become overwhelmed, isolated, afraid, or unable to manage a crisis alone, I pledge to seek appropriate help and to communicate with a trusted person whenever I am able.

I will take reasonable steps to maintain normal communication, protect important records, and keep my intentions clear. A period of silence, a missed update, technical failure, or inability to access this website must not be interpreted as a withdrawal or reversal of this pledge.

If I unexpectedly disappear, become incapacitated, am detained without normal communication, or die under unusual or unexplained circumstances, I want the circumstances preserved, documented, and independently investigated with care and without premature conclusions.

I make this pledge voluntarily, publicly, and in good faith as a personal statement of my present intent.

What I am committing to

  • Preserve my life and personal safety.
  • Seek help when a crisis exceeds what I can manage alone.
  • Communicate clearly with trusted people when possible.
  • Keep important records factual, dated, and preserved.
